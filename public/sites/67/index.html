What materials are used in your products?

We use industrial-grade materials including hardened steel, aircraft-grade aluminum, and reinforced composites. All materials are selected for durability and performance in demanding environments.

Yes, we specialize in custom industrial solutions. Our engineering team can work with you to develop products tailored to your specific requirements and applications.

Our products meet or exceed industry standards including ISO 9001, ANSI, and OSHA requirements. Specific certifications vary by product line.
