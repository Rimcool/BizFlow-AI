Heritage Questions

Answers about our vintage collection and craftsmanship

Are your products authentic vintage or reproductions?

We offer both authentic vintage pieces and carefully crafted reproductions. Each product is clearly marked with its origin story. Our reproductions are made using traditional techniques to capture the authentic feel of vintage candles , lamp , books.

How do you source your materials?

We source materials from reputable suppliers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. For restoration pieces, we often salvage materials from period-correct sources to maintain historical accuracy.

Do you offer restoration services?

Yes, we provide expert restoration services for vintage candles , lamp , books. Our craftsmen use period-appropriate techniques to preserve the integrity and value of your cherished pieces while ensuring they remain functional for modern use.

How do I care for vintage products?

Each piece comes with specific care instructions tailored to its materials and age. Generally, we recommend gentle cleaning with appropriate products, avoiding direct sunlight, and maintaining stable humidity levels to preserve your vintage items for years to come.
