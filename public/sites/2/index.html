Colorful Questions

Are the colors really that vibrant?

Yes! Our mini burgers feature premium, fade-resistant colors that maintain their vibrancy. We use special techniques to ensure the colors pop!

Absolutely! We offer custom color options for many of our products. Contact us to create your perfect color combination.
